The main challenge of this project is to design the constructs necessary to

1) collect information about individuals
2) use that information to match individuals in an evidence-based, scientifically backed way

After discussing the idea with ChatGPT, I have arrived at the following conclusions:

1) we shall base our matching algorithm on scientific information

2) we shall do so by investigating the following scientific fields & theories
    2.1) relationship science
    2.2) attachment theory
    2.3) conflict style
    2.4) big five
    2.5) core values
    2.6) relationship goals
    
3) in addition to basing our matching algorithm on established science, we shall use a continuously improved 
   AI to establish relationships between data we collect and relationship outcomes

We shall split these tasks as follows:

1) study relationship science
2) design questionnaires that reveal relevant information about individuals, in a quantifiable manner
3) determine how to vectorize the collected information, and how to calculate similarity between individuals (potentially an ML-project)
4) deploy
